About us

A family electrical contractor for Toowoomba.

John Gilbert Electrical was founded by John and Pat Gilbert in 1993 and is a family owned and operated business.

Licensed contractor

Master Electrician member.

Prior to commencing this company John and Pat operated as a partnership from 1976 after returning from 5 years working in Papua New Guinea. John started out as an electrical contractor in 1973 and has over 40 years’ experience in the industry working both in Australia and overseas.

John’s son Johnnie is also an accomplished electrician with 18 years’ experience and brings to the business a wealth of knowledge. Johnnie worked closely with his father completing his apprenticeship with John Gilbert Electrical before widening his horizons and gaining valuable experience working both in Australia and the United Kingdom (including completing work for the Royal Household).

John Gilbert Electrical covers all facets of electrical work, including domestic, commercial, industrial and data and communications, in Toowoomba and surrounding areas.

Contractor licence number 9693. Master Electrician member. Arctick licence 56668.

Our mission

Our mission is to provide expert, efficient and reliable electrical services to all our valued customers. We strive to deliver only the highest quality workmanship and always conduct ourselves in a fair, honest and ethical manner. Safety is our priority and we will always put the safety of our staff, customers and the community first. We are knowledgeable and innovative and will analyse all options in order to tailor the right solution for each varied electrical situation. We aim to provide exceptional customer service and seek to exceed our client’s expectations.
